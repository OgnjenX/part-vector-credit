# EXP003 protocol — audit-stage draft

This file records the design before code. It is **not frozen** and authorizes no held-out run.
`FROZEN_PROTOCOL.json` will be created only after development, source review, tests, parameter
calibration, and a pre-confirmation commit.

## Question

Can a pART-inspired outcome-selected representation plus SMART-derived learned top-down matching
cause local lower-level plasticity such that early dendritic state predicts later synaptic and
somatic change, without neuron-wise causal/error input?

## Task constraints

- frame-by-frame closed-loop BCI;
- random hidden `P+`/`P-` roles per seed;
- observable visual/task feedback after every action frame;
- delayed outcome with distractors and working-memory retention;
- hidden remap and opposite observable contexts;
- motor, apical, and local-plasticity pathways explicitly separate;
- development and held-out seeds generated from disjoint frozen seed roots.

## Planned six-phase timing

The phase state must be generated by the circuit computation, not by multiplying one vector by
six arbitrary gains.

1. **Selection/pre-action:** category and hypothesis compete; `H_{k,h}` reads learned `T` into
   apical `D` before outcome information exists.
2. **Action:** `B_h` through current `W_lower` drives soma and the environment.
3. **Sensory feedback:** the next visual/task state arrives and changes bottom-up/local activity.
4. **Match/mismatch:** bottom-up state and `T` determine match; gamma-like resonant timing or
   beta/reset/search follows.
5. **Outcome/reward:** scalar success/surprise updates structural/temporal credit and motivated
   gain; it does not provide a cell vector.
6. **Post-outcome plasticity:** stored local pre/post timing is integrated only under the permitted
   resonance gate; `T` and `W_lower` updates are recorded separately.

## Mandatory conditions

1. frozen/no learning;
2. random controller;
3. plain contextual bandit over fixed motor repertoire;
4. contextual bandit plus direct `B` copy to apical readout;
5. contextual bandit plus generic reward-gated Hebbian consolidation;
6. pART-inspired selection without learned top-down expectancy;
7. pART selection plus learned `T` but no top-down-induced local plasticity;
8. primary pART selection plus learned `T` plus SMART-derived resonance-gated lower plasticity;
9. primary without structural credit;
10. primary without working memory;
11. primary without motivated attention/reinforcement;
12. primary without reset/search;
13. primary without resonance gating;
14. primary with top-down neuron permutation;
15. primary with expectancy learning disabled;
16. primary with `T` learned but `T -> local plasticity` blocked;
17. primary with apical expression suppressed only after learning;
18. corrected Grossberg-compatible motor-basis plasticity control;
19. explicit neuron-wise vector-credit positive control.

Every condition must have a unique mechanism manifest and unit test. Conditions 7 and 16 may be
merged only if development proves they are semantically identical, in which case the protocol must
say so before freezing instead of reporting duplicate evidence.

## Primary measurements

- behavior before and after remap;
- initial and learned alignment/leakage of `B`, `T`, and `W_lower`;
- soma `S`, dendrite `D`, top-down expectation `T`, lower weight `W_lower`;
- selected category/hypothesis and held-hypothesis counterfactual soma;
- resonance/reset/category recruitment;
- task error, change in error, outcome, and scalar reward;
- soma-conditioned dendritic residual fitted without using the tested later endpoint;
- `corr(D_residual_early, W_late-W_early)`;
- `corr(D_residual_early, S_late-S_early)`;
- within-hypothesis versions of both longitudinal endpoints;
- phase-resolved role and residual alignment;
- causal mediation and paired ablation contrasts.

## Francioni-analysis mapping

The primary residual will follow Francioni's logic: fit dendritic magnitude from coincident somatic
magnitude on a prespecified training partition, then carry the residual definition to the held-out
events/window. Network-state conditioning may be added as a separately labeled analysis; it may not
erase the basic soma-conditioned endpoint. Events, windows, cross-fitting, z-scoring level, and
minimum-event rules will be frozen before confirmation.

## Outcome logic

The qualitative A–E classification and candidate effect floors are in `THEORY_AUDIT.md`. Numeric
thresholds remain provisional until development. Outcome A requires the full causal longitudinal
chain and specificity beyond generic Hebbian consolidation; instantaneous `corr(D,c)` can never
satisfy it. Failure of the positive control makes the experiment non-diagnostic. A generic-Hebbian
match yields Outcome B. A primary failure with a valid positive control yields Outcome C. An
unjustified theory-to-code link yields Outcome D. Indistinguishable proxy-driven effects yield E.

## Freeze barrier

Before any held-out seed is instantiated:

1. finish all implementation and information-boundary tests;
2. run development seeds only;
3. record every parameter change and failure;
4. choose thresholds and windows without confirmatory data;
5. create `FROZEN_PROTOCOL.json` with source hashes, exact condition list, seeds, parameters,
   metrics, statistics, and classifier;
6. commit and push the frozen protocol;
7. execute the held-out suite exactly once.

Any repair after inspection becomes EXP003b or EXP004.
