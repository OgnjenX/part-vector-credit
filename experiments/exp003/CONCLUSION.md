# EXP003 conclusion

## Current status: theory audit passed with an important scope limitation

No empirical EXP003 conclusion is available because implementation and simulations have not begun.

The primary-source audit found that SMART does specify a defensible causal route from learned
top-down expectation through match/gamma selection to cell-selective local STDP and changed future
postsynaptic response. The theoretical hard stop is therefore not forced.

However, pART does not specify the full equation-complete mapping from an outcome-selected causal
representation to hidden-role BCI motor neurons. EXP003 will test a pART-inspired, SMART-derived
cross-system composition. Even a positive result would establish sufficiency of that composition,
not prove that the published pART architecture already solves neuron-wise motor credit or that the
brain uses it.

No claim has yet been made about Francioni's longitudinal signature, remapping, context reversal,
apical causal necessity, generic Hebbian sufficiency, or the need for explicit vector credit.
