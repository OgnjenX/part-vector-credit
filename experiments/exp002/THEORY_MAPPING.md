## What published theory does and does not specify

ART does specify a local associative rule by which an active category learns the distributed
lower pattern that co-occurs with it and later emits a learned expectation. pART specifies how a
causal representation may be maintained and reinforced across delayed outcome. The papers do
**not** provide a published, equation-complete pathway joining pART's selected causal item to the
specific apical compartments of arbitrary BCI output neurons. EXP002 composes these published
motifs. A success establishes sufficiency of that composition; it cannot establish that pART
already contains this exact cellular mechanism without the stated extrapolation.
