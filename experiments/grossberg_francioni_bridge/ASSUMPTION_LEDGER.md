## Natural extensions versus new theory

- **Weak/natural extension:** A category can own a learned patterned expectation. That is explicit ART machinery.
- **Moderate extrapolation:** A pART-selected category can engage a SMART-like feedback circuit in association cortex (A1), and higher feedback can influence distal apical state (part of A5).
- **Strong new hypotheses:** arbitrary neuron-resolved topology learned from the available signals (A2), and conversion of that topology into behaviorally correct signed/magnitude plasticity rather than selective gating (A4).
- **Independent observation assumption:** the optical/calcium/residual forward model (A6). This is necessary to reproduce the experiment but is not itself a credit-assignment rule.

## Information audit for A2

The learner may legitimately have global task state, mismatch, reward, context, the selected H, lower-cell activity, and local pre/post timing. It does not have hidden role \(c_i\) or \(\partial E/\partial y_i\). An associative outstar can store whatever lower pattern was coactive with H. The unresolved problem is why the stored pattern becomes aligned with the arbitrary causal vector rather than with incidental activity.

Three logically distinct solutions must not be conflated:

1. **Selection:** a pre-existing motor pattern already aligns with the hidden vector; pART reinforces its H.
2. **Associative consolidation:** successful execution creates a role-aligned lower pattern, which H stores as T.
3. **Cellular instruction:** a neuron-specific signal changes individual cells toward the causal direction.

Grossberg clearly provides machinery for (1) and storage in (2). The origin of a sufficiently role-aligned target pattern in (2) remains under-specified. Supplying it directly would silently introduce (3).

## Dependency structure

```text
A1 (pART H owns SMART-like T)
 └─ A2 (T learns arbitrary neuron-level topology)      [killer]
     ├─ A3 (center/surround expresses opponent roles)
     └─ A5 (signal reaches measured apical/L1 circuit) [killer]
         ├─ A4 (local timing gives correct durable change) [killer]
         └─ A6 (calcium/residual preserves the signal)
```

A2 and A4 are theory-content assumptions. A5 is anatomical. A6 is observational. A failure of A6 would not show that cellular credit is absent; a failure of A2 or A4 would directly undermine this Grossbergian explanation of arbitrary role-specific credit.
