# Conclusion: where Grossberg ends and the bridge begins

## Result in one sentence

ART/pART/SMART are not demonstrably incapable of generating a Francioni-like cellular pattern, but the published theory is under-specified at the decisive neuron-resolved representation→apical→signed-plasticity bridge; claiming that pART already explains Francioni would overstate the sources.

## Compact bridge

```text
behavioral outcome
  ──[pART: explicit]──> maintained structural/temporal credit
  ──[pART/ART: explicit]──> selected causal representation H
  ──[ART expectation exists; pART→SMART binding extrapolated]──> learned T(k,i)
  ──[arbitrary RSC cell topology: new]──> relevant L5/apical targets
  ──[SMART apical entry explicit; NDNF/signed surround new]──> match center/surround
  ──[SMART: explicit]──> membrane/spike timing
  ──[SMART Eq. 5/6: explicit]──> local ΔW
  ──[SMART/generic biophysics: explicit]──> future ΔS
  ──[biophysical observation model: new]──> dendritic calcium
  ──[Francioni analysis: explicit statistic]──> soma-conditioned residual
```

## Explicitly contained arrows

1. pART structural/temporal credit selects and maintains relevant causal representations across delayed outcomes.
2. ART categories learn top-down expectations and use match/resonance versus mismatch/reset/search.
3. SMART contains learned higher→lower feedback reaching lower L1/L5 apical dendrites and a modulatory on-center/off-surround match circuit.
4. SMART local plasticity depends on presynaptic conductance and postsynaptic voltage/spike timing and changes future response.
5. Francioni's BCI causally defines cellular roles and its soma-conditioned dendritic residual is role-related, longitudinally predictive, and NDNF-sensitive.

## Missing or under-specified arrows

1. pART H is not explicitly bound to the SMART category/feedback projection that targets the relevant RSC population.
2. No audited Grossberg source shows how scalar outcome creates an arbitrary neuron-resolved T aligned with intermingled hidden P+/P− roles.
3. The on-center/off-surround motif is not shown to map behavioral role into graded, correctly signed lasting plasticity rather than match-selective eligibility.
4. SMART's modeled inhibitory surround is not identified with NDNF+ L1 inhibition at the Francioni compartment.
5. No source supplies the voltage/current/calcium/indicator forward model required to predict the soma-conditioned residual.

## Minimal extra assumptions

- **A1, moderate:** pART-selected H owns/selects a SMART-like context-specific expectation in RSC.
- **A2, strong:** that expectation learns arbitrary neuron-fine causal topology without a hidden vector.
- **A3, moderate–strong:** center/surround expresses the learned topology as cell-specific opponent membrane influence.
- **A4, strong:** local timing converts it into the behaviorally correct signed/magnitude plasticity and later soma.
- **A5, moderate–strong:** the pathway reaches the measured apical/NDNF circuit.
- **A6, moderate:** the biological signal survives the calcium and soma-residual observation transform.

A2 is presently weakest. It is the missing source of the vector structure, not a small parameterization detail.

## What EXP003 actually tested

EXP003a tested a central, source-derived SMART local arrow: a **supplied** matched top-down state changed membrane/spike timing, gated Eq. 5/6-derived plasticity, and altered future response. That narrow motif validated.

EXP003b then tested a larger composition whose crucial H→T→arbitrary BCI-cell mapping and dendritic observation model were engineered bridge assumptions. Frozen Outcome C remains unchanged: behavioral selection and T learning occurred, but the preregistered Francioni longitudinal residual endpoint failed while vector credit succeeded.

The frozen post-hoc analysis localized the first non-estimable modeled arrow at net local top-down influence→synthetic dendritic residual; raw T predicted sparse local ΔW, and ΔW predicted held-hypothesis future ΔS. This suggests the reduced observation model was a limiting bridge, but the severe sparsity prevents promotion to confirmatory success. EXP003 therefore tested one genuine SMART mechanism and, more importantly, one invented cross-system/measurement bridge.

## The three strongest biological tests

1. **Projection-specific, phase-specific top-down-source perturbation:** determine whether pre-outcome role information and later learning causally depend on a learned cortical feedback source while visual error/reward remain available.
2. **Within-cell context reversal/remapping:** test whether category/source feedback reorganizes first and gives the same neuron opposite pre-outcome apical signs before longitudinal soma change.
3. **Excitatory-feedback versus NDNF-surround dissection:** measure both currents and perturb them independently to ask whether an organized center/surround actually carries the opponent cellular pattern.

These directly target K1–K3. Broad perturbations that merely suppress all somatic firing are less discriminating.

## Single most useful next computational experiment

If another computation is pursued, it should be a **preregistered measurement-bridge and SMART-engagement dose-response**, not a new credit rule:

1. replay fixed, source-labeled excitatory top-down, inhibitory L1, feedforward, and somatic inputs;
2. use a compartmental L5 apical model with independently constrained calcium/indicator kinetics;
3. prespecify several input strengths/timings to avoid the frozen model's sparse/quantized regime;
4. generate dendritic and somatic fluorescence;
5. apply Francioni's analysis unchanged and localize information through T→current→voltage/timing→ΔW→ΔS→residual.

This tests A4/A6 and the post-hoc localization. It does not solve A2. If the neuron-resolved T target cannot be independently justified, biological source/remapping experiments are more informative than further BCI fitting.

## Sufficiency and attribution boundaries

**Future success could establish:** computational sufficiency of a precisely specified pART+SMART+A1–A6 composition; that an explicit neuron-wise propagated error is not logically necessary for that model to produce the measured pattern.

**Future success could not establish:** that published pART already contains the full explanation; that the brain uses this composition; that the signal is not an error; or that alternative cellular-credit mechanisms are false.

**Future failure could falsify:** the specified bridge, anatomy, timing rule, or observation model under the tested regime. A source-specific biological failure of K1–K3 would be stronger than a simulation failure.

**Future failure could not falsify:** ART, pART, SMART, Grossberg's stability–plasticity theory, or all representation-based credit accounts globally.

**Remaining unresolved either way:** the biological identity of H/T, how arbitrary neuron-level topology is acquired from the animal's available signals, whether the dendritic residual is graded instruction or selective modulation, and whether another local/global learning mechanism better explains the data.

## Recommended decision

Do not add a cellular-credit mechanism solely because EXP003b failed. First test the anatomical and observational bridge. But do not describe the missing H→arbitrary-cell mapping as already supplied by Grossberg: unless A2 can be derived or observed, a model that adds it is a new hybrid hypothesis.
