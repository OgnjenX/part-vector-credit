# Killer bridge assumptions

These assumptions are indispensable: if any is biologically false, this Grossbergian explanation of Francioni collapses even though ART/pART/SMART may remain valid for their original domains.

## K1 — A learned causal representation reaches the right cells and compartment

**Claim.** The pART-relevant H owns/selects a learned expectation with enough cellular resolution to target the arbitrary, intermingled RSC BCI neurons, and this signal reaches the measured L1/apical circuit.

**Why indispensable.** Without neuron-resolved H→T→apical connectivity, structural credit cannot become a cellular pattern. A broad representation- or spatial-level feedback signal cannot explain opposite residuals in nearby cells solely by being “top-down.”

**Evidence status.** Category-owned expectations and higher→L1 feedback are **SOURCE-DERIVED** in ART/SMART separately. Their binding to arbitrary RSC roles is **CROSS-SYSTEM EXTRAPOLATION/NEW HYPOTHESIS** A1/A2/A5.

**Killer test.** Identify and silence the learned/contextual top-down source. If complete suppression leaves the cell-role residual and relearning intact while sensory error and reward are preserved, the proposed bridge fails.

## K2 — Match dynamics determine the behaviorally correct local plasticity

**Claim.** Center/surround and SMART timing do more than gate eligible cells: they produce the correct signed and sufficiently graded local change that predicts later soma.

**Why indispensable.** Francioni's key longitudinal result cannot be explained by a pattern that is merely expressed apically but has no causal relation to future cellular change.

**Evidence status.** SMART timing-dependent plasticity is **SOURCE-DERIVED** and EXP003a validated a reduced motif. Mapping arbitrary causal role to the required update direction/magnitude is **NEW HYPOTHESIS** A4.

**Killer test.** Hold H/T fixed and perturb timing through the SMART window. If role-correct ΔW and future ΔS do not track the predicted timing law—or occur equally when T is blocked from the local plasticity circuit—this bridge fails.

## K3 — The measured dendritic/NDNF phenomenon is an expression of this pathway

**Claim.** The candidate feedback and its inhibitory interaction generate the biological calcium signal that survives soma conditioning, and their contribution is specifically required for learning.

**Why indispensable.** A mechanism can alter hidden synapses yet fail to explain the empirical phenomenon. Conversely, an observed residual generated elsewhere cannot be attributed to Grossbergian feedback.

**Evidence status.** SMART's apical feedback and Francioni's NDNF dependence are separately **SOURCE-DERIVED**. Their identity is a **NEW HYPOTHESIS** A5/A6.

**Killer test.** Simultaneously measure source axons, apical current/voltage/calcium, soma, and residual during pathway perturbation. If the residual does not depend on or encode the candidate pathway, attribution fails.

## Weakest current assumption

K1/A2 is the weakest and most speculative: none of the audited sources shows how scalar outcome plus pART structural credit learns an arbitrary, context-specific, neuron-resolved expectation over intermingled BCI cells. Grossberg provides associative storage of an active lower pattern, but the theory does not yet explain why the stored pattern has the hidden behavioral sign structure rather than reflecting pre-existing basis alignment or incidental covariance.
