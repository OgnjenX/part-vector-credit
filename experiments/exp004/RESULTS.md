# EXP004 results

Held-out confirmation: **not yet executed**.

Development results are used only for calibration and protocol freezing. They showed:

- controlled initial coverage strongly covaried with behavior and learned T;
- T was exactly reconstructible and almost identical to its selected-h initial pattern/target average;
- outcome-shuffled and random-credit controls failed, confirming genuine scalar-outcome information acquisition;
- full ART did not consistently outperform a contextual bandit and was inferior to a two-category no-recruitment ablation on development mappings;
- the phase-composition learner exceeded the repeated-single oracle and approached the allowed-sequence oracle;
- the generic scalar motor extension did not produce a robust normalized-behavior advantage after calibration, while the hidden-vector control solved the task.

These are not confirmatory claims. This file will be completed exactly once after frozen confirmation.
