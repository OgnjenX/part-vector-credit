# EXP005 statistical analysis plan

Status: **PRE-REGISTERED BEFORE HELD-OUT RUN**

The unit of replication is the random hidden-mapping seed. Means and paired
condition differences use 5,000 deterministic nonparametric bootstrap replicates.
All comparisons are paired by seed and population size.

The binary generic-mechanism decision uses N=32 only and the floors in
`PROTOCOL.md`. Scaling at N=8/16/64 is descriptive. The source-theory outcome is
already E and cannot be changed by simulation.

Construction is quantified three ways:

1. final minus initial alignment;
2. final minus best single exploratory sample;
3. exact reconstruction from all legal local variables and scalar outcome.

The second endpoint is deliberately stringent. A negative value means the stable
learner did not outperform the luckiest transient sample; it does not erase de novo
learning if final topology remains far above chance and exactly integrates many
legal updates.

Local information is assessed by per-neuron correlations between eligibility and
later true outcome, followed by correlation of that vector with the offline hidden
role. This is diagnostic of where the scalar reward obtains cell-specific
information; hidden role is used only in analysis.

No post-hoc subset can convert a failed preregistered classification into success.
No claim of ART specificity will be made because no ART/Grossberg primary model is
run.
