## Killer missing assumptions

The hard-stop audit turns on two inseparable new assumptions:

1. each RSC coordinate supplies a retained, locally accessible exploratory
   eligibility;
2. later scalar outcome modulates that eligibility with the correct bidirectional
   sign.

Together they are the cellular-credit algorithm. Neither can be introduced as a
minor implementation convenience.
