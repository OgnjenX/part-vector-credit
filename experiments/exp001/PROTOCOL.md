# EXP001 — De novo causal-credit experiment

Status: criteria frozen before development execution on 2026-08-22. Development
seeds are 0–7; held-out confirmatory seeds are 1000–1029. No confirmatory threshold
may be changed after inspecting held-out results.

## Question and information boundary

Can published Grossberg-style structural/temporal credit, motivated attention,
reinforcement gating, and hypothesis search produce a neuron-specific dendritic
signature when the learner has no neuron-wise causal information?

For every seed, the environment randomly assigns exchangeable neurons to hidden
P+ and P− populations. It alone computes `u = mean(P+) - mean(P-)`, updates a
seven-bin grating state, and evaluates improvement/success. The learner can observe
context, target, grating/error, its own activity and selected hypothesis, delayed
global improvement, and scalar reward. It cannot access P+/P− identity, the causal
vector, a per-neuron target, or an equivalent derivative. Hidden roles are redrawn
after the remap boundary without changing observable identities.

An action is followed by four intervening distractors and only then by outcome.
Working memory must retain the selected causal hypothesis across that interval.
Somatic activity is generated by the learner's chosen control pattern; it is never
conditioned on the correct hidden population.

## Mechanism claims

The Grossberg-only claim is limited to selection and reinforcement among random,
pre-existing distributed control hypotheses. The full condition never changes the
neuron-wise signs of that bank. A separately labeled outstar-like plastic-basis
condition consolidates executed activity but receives no causal direction; it is
an engineering probe, not claimed as a published solution to the hidden mapping.
The explicit-vector condition receives the forbidden causal derivative and is only
a task-learnability positive control.

## Conditions

Primary conditions are: frozen zero-plasticity; random policy/feedback; full
pART-inspired abstraction; random structural credit; no working memory; no
motivated attention; no Now Print/reinforcement gate; no reset/search; no
match/resonance gate; shuffled top-down feedback; frozen random-basis selection;
plastic basis; apical suppression; and explicit vector-error positive control.
Every condition is run with identical environment seeds.

Hidden causal remapping occurs at 60% of training. Capacity is swept over 2, 8, 32,
128, and 512 hypotheses. Robustness sweeps vary vigilance, attention gain, memory
persistence, reinforcement rate, exploration, and capacity. Generalization probes
vary population size/balance, causal strength/noise, partial remapping, stochastic
reward, delayed outcome, changed target, and two observable contexts with opposing
causal roles.

## Frozen primary criteria

All criteria apply to averages over held-out seeds unless explicitly seed-level.

1. **No initialization leakage:** mean signed template–causal correlation has
   absolute value < 0.05; nearest-centroid leave-one-neuron-out label decoding is
   not above its 200-permutation null at group level (`p >= 0.05`). Individual
   random patterns may align by chance; maximum alignment is reported as capacity.
2. **Task validity:** explicit-vector control has late success ≥ 0.70 and improves
   by ≥ 0.25 over its first evaluation window.
3. **No prelearning solution:** frozen and random-policy late success < 0.60 and
   improve by < 0.10.
4. **Grossberg-only behavioral learning:** full late success ≥ 0.70, improves by
   ≥ 0.20, and exceeds frozen by ≥ 0.15.
5. **Relearning:** post-remap late success ≥ 0.65 and exceeds the first 20% of the
   remap phase by ≥ 0.15.
6. **Vector signature:** the mean P+ signal is positive and mean P− signal negative
   in ≥ 80% of seeds; mean role-alignment ≥ 0.20; early residual
   alignment predicts later per-neuron somatic change with mean correlation ≥ 0.20.
7. **Causal pathway test:** apical suppression reduces residual role-alignment by
   ≥ 50% and late/relearning success by ≥ 0.15 while changing the environment's
   passive sensory state representation by < 0.05 standardized units.

If full succeeds only as capacity increases with the best initial random alignment,
the result is Outcome 2 (pre-existing basis selection), not strong sufficiency.
If the positive control succeeds but Grossberg-only fails, it is Outcome 4. If the
proxies dominate interpretation, the result is Outcome 5.
