# EXP003b conclusion

## Outcome C — composition fails despite a validated SMART motif

EXP003b falsifies the strong Grossbergian hypothesis **for this frozen
mechanism-level composition**. Scalar delayed outcome successfully selected useful
distributed hypotheses and learned initially uninformative, role-aligned top-down
expectations. Those expectations genuinely altered the validated SMART-derived
local spike timing and lower weights. Yet the early soma-conditioned dendritic
signal did not predict which lower weights or future same-hypothesis somatic
responses changed. This failure repeated after hidden causal remapping.

The exact source of the patterned apical signal was ART-outstar sampling of the
centered lower activity coactive with a selected category-hypothesis, amplified by
learned scalar value. It was not a propagated gradient and never received hidden
`c`. This mechanism explained structured, remappable top-down activity, but not
Francioni's learning-dependent cellular relationship.

A direct-copy bandit generated at least as much instantaneous causal-role alignment
as the primary learned expectancy, while generating no longitudinal change. This
confirms that a Francioni-looking patterned readout can arise from generic
selection/routing and is not specifically evidence for pART/SMART. Conversely, the
generic scalar-gated Hebbian learner changed weights and behavior but also failed
the full longitudinal chain. Neither simpler control produced Outcome B.

The critical `T→SMART` block removed local weight change, but it could not
selectively abolish a longitudinal effect that was absent in the primary model.
The expression-only perturbation left the motor projection and learned weights
intact and retained nonzero behavior, yet it changed post-remap performance. Thus
motor and apical branches are distinct, while modeled top-down modulation is not
behaviorally inert at expression.

After remapping, old `T` became inappropriate and a new role-aligned `T` emerged;
behavior relearned above frozen/random controls. Opposing contexts did not produce
the preregistered strength of opposite expectations. The architecture therefore
showed representation-level temporal/structural credit and remappable feedback,
not adequate cell-level parametric credit.

Only the explicitly neuron-wise positive control reproduced the complete frozen
`D→W→S` assay. Within this tested model, an additional cell-specific credit source
is required to explain the Francioni longitudinal signature. This does **not**
prove that biological cortex uses backpropagation, that Francioni's signal is a
gradient, or that every possible Grossberg architecture fails. It shows that the
published motifs as composed here do not contain enough information to determine
the needed cell-specific changes from scalar outcome. A different local mechanism
would be a new, independently justified hybrid hypothesis and should be tested as
a new experiment, not added post hoc to EXP003b.
